Why I’m building this

I work as a server in Salt Lake City, so I see what a kitchen looks like when the truck arrives. The invoice gets signed, put on a spike or in a folder, and rarely compared with last week’s.

Yet everything an owner needs to know about price rises is printed on that paper. Finding it means sitting with a stack of invoices and a spreadsheet, and nobody has that hour.

The software that does this well is built and priced for restaurant groups with a finance team. I’m building InvoicePlate for the places I actually work in: one location, an owner who does the books at night.

For now I read every invoice myself. That’s on purpose. I want to learn what owners need before I automate any of it.

If you run a restaurant in Salt Lake and this sounds useful, send me twenty invoices. I’ll show you what I find.
