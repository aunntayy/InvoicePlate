# Owner and manager interview script

About 20 minutes. Goal: learn how they handle supplier invoices today and whether price creep costs them money they would pay to get back. You are not pitching until the last section.

Rules:
- Ask about what they did last time, not what they would do. "When did you last notice a price went up?" beats "Would you want alerts?"
- Let silences sit. Don't fill them with the product.
- Write down exact words, especially dollar amounts and complaints.
- If they say "that would be nice," ask what they did about it last time. No action usually means no pain.

## 1. Context (2 min)

- How many locations? Roughly how many covers a week?
- Who orders from suppliers, and who receives deliveries?
- Which suppliers do you use most? Roughly how many invoices a week?

## 2. How invoices are handled today (5 min)

- Walk me through what happens to an invoice after the truck leaves. Who touches it, and where does it end up?
- Does anyone compare the prices to last time? How? (Spreadsheet, memory, the bookkeeper, nobody.)
- What software, if any, do you use for invoices, inventory or food cost? What do you pay for it?
- Does your bookkeeper or accountant see the invoices? What do they do with them?

## 3. The pain (8 min)

- Tell me about the last time a price went up and you noticed. How did you find out? How long after?
- Was there a time you found out late? What did it cost you, roughly?
- Have you been short-shipped or charged for something you didn't get? How did you catch it?
- Ever paid the same invoice twice?
- What's your food cost percentage? How do you know? How often do you check it?
- If you could know one thing about your supplier costs every week, what would it be?

## 4. Reaction (3 min)

Only now, describe it in one sentence: "You forward or photograph your invoices, and once a week you get a short summary of which prices went up and what that cost you."

- What would you do with that summary?
- Who else would need to see it?
- What would make you stop reading it after a month?
- What would worry you about sending us your invoices?

## 5. The ask (2 min)

- "I'm running a free pilot with a few Salt Lake restaurants. You'd send me your last 20 or so invoices (photos are fine), and I'd send back a report within a few days. Every report is checked by me before you get it. Want in?"
- If yes: agree on how they'll send them (text, email, or I pick them up) and a date.
- If no: "What would need to be true for this to be worth your time?"
- Either way: "Who else should I talk to?"
