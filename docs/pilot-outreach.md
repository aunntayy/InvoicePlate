# Pilot outreach messages

For asking restaurant owners and managers for a 20-minute chat and a free pilot. Replace the [brackets]. Keep every version short, and don't add numbers we can't back up.

## In person (after a shift, or with someone you know)

"I'm building a small tool for restaurants on the side. It reads your supplier invoices and tells you which prices went up and what that cost you. Could I ask you 20 minutes of questions about how you handle invoices now? No sales pitch. I'm trying to find out if this is worth building."

## Text or DM (warm contact)

> Hi [name], it's [your name] from [restaurant / how you know them]. I'm working on a side project that tracks supplier prices from invoices, so owners see when something like chicken or produce goes up and what it costs them. Could I buy you a coffee and ask 20 minutes of questions about how you handle invoices? Not selling anything yet.

## Email (cold)

**Subject:** Supplier price increases at [restaurant]

> Hi [name],
>
> I'm [your name]. I work as a server in Salt Lake, and I'm building InvoicePlate, a simple tool that reads supplier invoices and flags price increases, short deliveries and duplicate charges.
>
> I'm looking for a few local restaurants for a free pilot: you send me your last 20 or so invoices (photos are fine), and within a few days I send back a report on which prices moved and what that cost you. I check every report by hand before it goes out.
>
> Before that, I'd value 20 minutes of your time to hear how you handle invoices today. Would [day] or [day] work?
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]
> [phone]

## What to say about their data

If they ask (or before they send anything):

- "I store the supplier name, invoice number, date and the line items (item, quantity, unit, price). Nothing else."
- "It's stored encrypted, used only to make your report, and never sold."
- "Say the word and I'll delete all of it."

Once AI extraction is added, invoice images are sent to the AI provider to read them. Say so here before that goes live.

## Follow-up (no reply after 4-5 days)

> Hi [name], following up on my note about supplier prices. If now's a bad time, no problem. Is there someone else at [restaurant] who handles invoices I could talk to?

Send one follow-up only.
